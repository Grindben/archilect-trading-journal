A three-stage filter that runs before every session: score the market conditions, resolve the daily bias, then grade the setup itself.

Each stage feeds the next. Conditions can stop the day outright, the bias sets direction and conviction, and only then does a setup earn a grade and a risk size.

Every trade is logged in the journal, so the record is built as you go rather than reconstructed afterwards.
